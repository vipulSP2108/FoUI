# Renovated Medical Centre Fieldwork & Observation Checklist

**Target Site:** Newly Renovated IITGN Medical Centre (Current Campus Facility)  
**Primary Investigator Group:** Group M  
**Execution Purpose:** Gathering primary empirical UX data to compare with the historical baseline  

---

## 1. Fieldwork Objective & Methodological Protocol

The goal of this fieldwork is to map the physical ergonomics, spatial circulation, signage visibility, and waste disposal behaviors in the **newly renovated Medical Centre building**. 

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Ethical & Safety Protocol:** Under no circumstances should Group M members physically handle, open, or tamper with bio-medical waste bags. All observations are strictly visual, non-contact, and conducted without photographing patient or staff faces.

---

## 2. Comprehensive Fieldwork Checklist

### Phase A: Spatial & Architectural Mapping
* [ ] **Map the Floor Plan:** Sketch or obtain the exact layout of the renovated facility. Identify door openings, patient corridors, and clinical workstations.
* [ ] **Identify Key Functional Rooms:**
  * [ ] Triage & Vitals Station
  * [ ] Outpatient Consultation Cabins (OPD Cabins 1, 2, 3)
  * [ ] Minor Operation Theatre (OT) / Dressing & Procedure Room
  * [ ] Specimen & Blood Sample Collection Room
  * [ ] Pharmacy & Medication Dispensing Counter
  * [ ] Observation Ward / Inpatient Recovery Area
  * [ ] Central Waiting Lounge & Registration Desk
* [ ] **Measure Ergonomic Distances:** Measure walking distance (metres and steps) from:
  * Consultation chair to waste bin.
  * Examination/procedure cot to waste bin.
  * Dressing trolley to waste bin.
  * Blood draw chair to sharps disposal box.

### Phase B: Bin Inventory & Environmental Auditing
* [ ] **Bin Color & Category Audit:** For each room, record the exact presence and condition of:
  * Yellow Bin (Infectious/Pathological waste)
  * Red Bin (Contaminated plastic, tubing, gloves)
  * Blue Bin (Glassware, ampoules, vials)
  * White / Translucent Puncture-Proof Sharps Box
  * Black / Green Bin (General municipal solid waste)
* [ ] **Luminance & Contrast Assessment:**
  * Note lighting quality (natural daylight vs. overhead LED panels). Are bins located in shadow zones?
  * Observe wall paint color behind bins (e.g., white, off-white, beige) and assess visual pop vs. blending.
* [ ] **Physical Accessibility & Mechanism:**
  * Foot pedal condition: Does the pedal open smoothly, or does it stick, forcing users to use hands?
  * Aperture clearance: Can bulky dressing pads be inserted without touching contaminated bin rims?
  * Sharps box mounting: Is it mounted on a wall bracket at eye level, placed on a moving trolley, or sitting on the floor?

### Phase C: Contextual Observation of User Interactions (30+ events per room)
* [ ] **Clinical Staff (Doctors & Nurses):**
  * Observe disposal hesitation: Do staff pause ($>2$ seconds) before selecting a bin?
  * Look for head turns and bag-lifting behaviors (checking bag color inside the bin).
  * Record disposal "slips" (e.g., throwing gloves into the general waste bin during rush moments).
* [ ] **Custodial Personnel (Housekeeping):**
  * Shadow one complete bag clearance cycle (morning or evening).
  * Record how bags are sealed, tied, lifted, and transported.
  * Note how replacement polybags are sourced and whether color mismatches occur.
* [ ] **Patients & Non-Medical Visitors:**
  * Observe disposal behaviors in waiting corridors and reception areas.
  * Record instances of food packaging, coffee cups, or tissues thrown into biomedical bins.
