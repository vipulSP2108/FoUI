# Comparative Evaluation Matrix: Old vs. Renovated vs. Proposed AI-AMWS

**Document Purpose:** Longitudinal comparative synthesis across all physical, visual, and operational UX dimensions  
**Comparison Entities:**
1. **Historical Baseline:** Pre-renovation IITGN Medical Centre (2024–2025 facility data)
2. **Renovated Facility (Post-Renovation):** Current IITGN Medical Centre setup
3. **Proposed Design Evolution:** AI-Powered Adaptive Medical Waste System (AI-AMWS)

---

## Comprehensive Multi-Dimensional Matrix

| Evaluation Dimension | Historical Baseline (Old Setup) | Current Renovated Facility (Post-Renovation) | Proposed Evolution: AI-AMWS |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Spatial Geometry & Footprint** | Cramped clinical rooms; narrow shared corridor; bins pushed into awkward corner nooks. | Redesigned partitions and wider corridors, but interior bin placement was omitted from the plan; bins pushed into random corners. | AI-guided spatial positioning based on clinical traffic heatmaps and reach ergonomics. |
| **Ergonomic Distance to Bins** | Variable (1.5 m to 4.2 m); clinicians frequently had to turn $180^\circ$ and walk across the room. | Improved corridor width, but procedure bins clustered under sample counters or in far room corners (8 red-dot locations). | Optimized line-of-sight placement; point-of-care modular docking next to procedure carts. |
| **Visual Ergonomics & Contrast** | Severe environmental blending; yellow/red bins blended with beige tiles ($\Delta E > 12$ fading). | Management explicitly painted walls off-white/light after patient visibility complaints; yet bins remain generic, unlabelled buckets. | High-contrast backplates with proximity-triggered chromatic NeoPixel LED aperture rings. |
| **Signage & Signifiers** | Faded, peeling paper stickers; small English-only text; handwritten polybag markers. | Zero category labels on interior bins; severe polybag mismatches (yellow bag in blue bin); staff rely on verbal briefings to patients. | Ambient aperture lighting matching official colors + instant multi-modal visual confirmation. |
| **Capacity Management** | Static, rigid 30L/20L bins; frequent afternoon overflows in Treatment Room (15:00–17:00). | Unpredictable, non-clock surge hours; Minor OT bins overflow erratically while consultation bins sit nearly empty. | Predictive surge forecasting (24–48h) + modular dynamic capacity rebalancing across rooms. |
| **Point-of-Disposal Feedback** | Zero system feedback; users rely entirely on manual visual confirmation inside open bag. | Zero system feedback; open lidless buckets or broken swing flaps (`20261110_120649.jpg.jpeg`). | Real-time dual feedback: gentle LED color pulse confirming disposal; amber warning for capacity. |
| **Maintenance & Custodial Workflow** | Rigid clock-based collection (morning/evening); full bins left unserviced during peak afternoons. | Two-tier manual logistics: reactive phone calls to housekeeping for internal room-to-balcony transfer; morning pickup by campus plant. | Real-time dynamic dispatch via custodial mobile app; route optimization based on fill & 48h limit. |
| **Intermediate Buffering** | None; waste remained in clinical rooms until scheduled evening removal. | Exterior balcony buffer trolley with 4 CPCB bins (`outsidebins.jpeg`) acting as intermediate staging point before campus truck pickup. | IoT-monitored buffer staging with automated fill alerts linked to campus waste processing schedules. |
| **Item Mis-Segregation Rates** | High (18–25% mis-segregation observed in observation wards and corridors). | Extreme point-of-care violations: syringe wrappers in red buckets, uncapped needles in pink buckets, soiled cotton in beige buckets. | Near-zero target ($<3\%$) through unambiguous visual guidance and optional CV item check. |
| **Cognitive Load Under Stress** | High; requires conscious recall of color categories and visual deciphering of faded labels. | Severe; clinicians and patients must decipher unmarked domestic buckets or rely on memorized verbal briefings. | Minimal; intuitive, pre-attentive chromatic signifiers guide subconscious muscle memory. |
